Volunteer in La Concepción, Nicaragua

Live locally. Share English. Make an impact.

Join Expressive English Academy for a meaningful cultural exchange in La Concepción, Masaya. In exchange for 4 hours of volunteer support per day, we provide free accommodation and breakfast during your stay.

Apply by WhatsApp See the exchange

What the exchange looks like

Free accommodation A place to stay in La Concepción during your volunteer experience.
Breakfast included Start your day with breakfast provided as part of your stay.
4 hours of support per day 2 hours in the morning and 2 hours in the afternoon.

A simple and meaningful exchange

This program is designed for people who want more than tourism. You get to experience daily life in a Nicaraguan community while helping students gain confidence in English.

What we provide

Your stay with us

Accommodation at no cost during your agreed volunteer stay. Breakfast included. A local experience in La Concepción, Masaya. Direct interaction with students, families and the academy community. A chance to experience Nicaragua beyond the usual tourist route.
What you give

Your time and your English

4 hours of volunteer support per day. 2 hours in the morning and 2 hours in the afternoon. Conversation practice with English learners. Support with pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar. Participation in classroom and conversation activities. Encouragement that helps students feel more confident speaking English.

What would you actually do?

You do not need to arrive with a complicated lesson plan. Your biggest value is giving students authentic opportunities to use English with another person.

Conversation

Talk with students

Help students practice real conversations, ask questions, tell stories and become more comfortable using English outside of a textbook.

Language support

Explain English naturally

Support students with grammar, pronunciation, vocabulary and common expressions when they need clarification or extra practice.

Cultural exchange

Share your world

Talk about your culture, traditions, travel experiences and everyday life while also learning about Nicaragua and our community.

Your daily schedule

The exact activities may vary depending on student schedules and academy needs, but the daily commitment is structured around two separate sessions.

Morning

2 hours

Conversation practice, English support, small-group activities or helping students review language they are currently learning.

Afternoon

2 hours

Additional conversation sessions, classroom support, pronunciation practice, grammar help or cultural-exchange activities.

Who is a good fit?

We are looking for volunteers who understand that meaningful exchange is built on consistency, respect and genuine interest in helping others learn.

Communication

Comfortable using English

You should be able to communicate clearly in English and enjoy speaking with learners at different levels.

Attitude

Patient and respectful

Our students are learning. Patience, kindness, reliability and a supportive attitude matter more than being a professional teacher.

Community

Open to local life

This experience is best for someone who wants to connect with people, learn about Nicaragua and participate respectfully in a local community.

Important

This is a non-cash volunteer exchange

Expressive English Academy does not provide cash payment or a salary for this volunteer role. The exchange is accommodation and breakfast in return for the agreed 4 hours of volunteer support per day.

Volunteers are responsible for their own transportation to and from Nicaragua, personal expenses, additional meals unless otherwise agreed, travel insurance and any immigration or entry requirements that apply to their trip.

Interested?

If you enjoy meeting people, sharing your language and discovering a community from the inside, we would love to hear from you. Tell us where you are from, when you would like to visit and how long you hope to stay.
